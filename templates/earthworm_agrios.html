Earthworms Are Beneficial!

Earthworms are nature's soil engineers. They improve soil structure, enhance nutrient availability, and increase water infiltration. Their presence indicates healthy, fertile soil.

Why Earthworms Are Good for Your Farm

Soil Aeration

Create channels that improve air and water movement in soil

Nutrient Cycling

Break down organic matter and make nutrients available to plants

Water Retention

Improve soil's ability to hold and distribute water

Organic Matter

Produce nutrient-rich castings that fertilize the soil naturally
